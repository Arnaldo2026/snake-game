# Game Room – versions

The version is in the `VERSION` file and shows on the home screen (bottom), next to the commit it was built from.
Bump it with every change pushed: the last number for fixes, the middle one for new features or games.

## 1.4.0 – 2026-11-08
Poker: default is now Five-card draw with the Italian short deck. Words of the game explained on demand:
tap a move badge, the D button or the pot on the table to see what it means; "📘 Terms" opens a glossary
(dealer/cartaro, blinds/buio and controbuio, check/bussa, call/vedi-sto, raise, fold/lascia-passo, all-in,
stand pat/servito, side pot, kicker, pot odds, Italian deck…) with this table's facts: who deals, that play
goes clockwise, who speaks first this round. The glossary is also in the manual (English and Italian).

## 1.3.0 – 2026-11-08
Poker: a coloured badge beside every player with their last move (fold, check, call, raise, all-in, change N),
"in pot" on each name plate and the list of this hand's moves round by round. New room settings: Deck –
52 cards or Italian short deck (lowest card = 11 − players; flush beats full house; A-7-8-9-10 straight with
4 players) – and Computer moves – automatic or step by step (each computer move announced, with its
reasoning in Advice/Demo, waiting for Continue). Manual updated in both languages.

## 1.2.0 – 2026-11-08
Poker in English or Italian: each player picks their language in the room (table, buttons, advice, hand
names, results, room settings and the manual). After each hand the result stays on screen (host setting
8 / 15 / 30 s or until everyone is ready, with a Next hand button): winner, hand, why it won (e.g. kicker),
every hand shown with its best five cards highlighted; "Last hand" shows it again during the next one.
Computer players think a little longer. Platform: per-device language for games that offer it,
translated room settings.

## 1.1.0 – 2026-11-08
New game: Poker – Texas Hold'em or Five-card draw (host's choice), 2–6 players; alone you play against
1–5 computer players. Same starting chips for all, blinds, a cap on raises per betting round, side pots,
time to act, matches of 10/20 hands or last one standing. Modes: Normal, Advice (your hand, chance to win,
pot odds, suggested move, cards to change) and Demo (all cards face up, advice for everyone).
"How to play" manual with the ranking of hands (room screen and 📖 in the game).
Platform: per-player state (hidden cards never leave the server), a game action panel, board clicks,
game keyboard shortcuts and the manual window.

## 1.0.1 – 2026-10-21
Version number easier to find: a badge in the bottom-right corner on PCs, at the end of the page on phones.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-20
First numbered version. Includes Snake, Space Wars, Asteroids, Lunar Lander, Battlezone, Pong Tennis and
Breakout; public/private rooms; Space Wars arena shapes, reverse thrust and the Thumbs and Slide phone layouts.
